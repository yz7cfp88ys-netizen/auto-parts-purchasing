BEFORE YOU START

A few useful answers.

Can I buy a small quantity or mix different parts?

Small-batch and mixed-product purchasing are part of the service. Minimum quantities vary by product and supplier. Share your list so availability and practical order quantities can be checked.

What information helps identify the right part?

Include an OEM or reference number, make, model, engine specification, production year and quantity. Photos, dimensions and existing labels are useful references. Fitment must be confirmed before ordering.

Can you source directly from factories?

Factory sourcing is available for larger requirements. Product specifications, minimum quantities, sample arrangements, production lead times and any customization need individual confirmation.

What does an inspection cover?

Inspection scope is agreed for each order. It can include quantity, appearance, labels, packaging or agreed specification checks. Testing, certification and third-party inspection should be discussed separately.

How are shipping costs and delivery times calculated?

They depend on destination, packed dimensions, weight, cargo type, route and shipping terms. A final arrangement must be confirmed for each shipment. Import duties and destination requirements also need review.

How do mixed orders and payments work?

On our PHP website, add different part references to one cart and submit one procurement order. We verify supply and send one complete quotation. Confirm the latest total before paying the whole order through PayPal. The static preview cannot accept orders or payments.
